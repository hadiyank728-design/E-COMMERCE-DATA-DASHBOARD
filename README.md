✨ About the Project

The E-Commerce Sales Dashboard is an interactive data analytics project developed using Microsoft Excel.

It transforms raw e-commerce transaction data into meaningful business insights using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPIs, Slicers, Timelines, and Data Visualization.

The dashboard provides a simple and interactive way to understand sales performance, profitability, customer behaviour, product performance, discounts, and regional trends.

⸻

🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

* 📈 Monitor overall sales performance
* 💰 Analyze profit and profit margin
* 🛍️ Identify high-performing products and categories
* 👥 Understand customer purchasing behaviour
* 🎯 Analyze the effect of discounts on profitability
* 🌍 Compare regional and state-wise performance
* 💳 Analyze payment methods and sales channels
* 📅 Track monthly sales and profit trends
* 📊 Support data-driven business decisions

⸻

📊 Dashboard Highlights

🔹 KPI Cards

The dashboard includes important KPIs such as:

KPI	Purpose
💰 Total Sales	Measures overall revenue
📈 Total Profit	Measures business profitability
🧾 Total Orders	Measures number of orders
📦 Units Sold	Measures products sold
💹 Profit Margin	Measures profitability percentage
🛒 AOV	Measures Average Order Value

⸻

📌 Key Analysis

📅 Sales Trend

Monthly Sales, Profit and Growth % are analyzed to identify business trends.

🏆 Product Performance

Identifies Top 10 and Bottom 10 products based on sales, quantity and profit.

🗂️ Category Analysis

Compares different product categories based on Sales and Profit.

👥 Customer Analysis

Identifies the Top 10 Customers based on sales and order performance.

🎯 Discount Analysis

Analyzes whether higher discounts lead to increased sales while reducing profitability.

🌍 Regional Analysis

Compares sales performance across Regions and States.

💳 Payment & Sales Channel

Analyzes customer preferences for different payment methods and sales channels.
